About the System

This Online Disaster Report Management System was designed and implemented to replace the existing manual paper-based record keeping system at Cross River State Fire Service, Calabar. The system provides a fast, secure, and centralized web-based platform for reporting, tracking, managing and archiving fire disaster incidents across Cross River State, Nigeria.

Key Features

Easy Report Submission

Submit fire disaster reports online from anywhere with photo and video evidence attached.

Role-Based Access

Separate dashboards for reporters, field responders and administrators with specific permissions for each role.

Real-Time Notifications

Automatic alerts notify administrators and responders instantly when a new report is submitted.

Instant Record Retrieval

Search and retrieve any past disaster record within seconds by date, location or report ID.

Statistics and Reports

Generate monthly and annual statistical summaries and charts for management and government agencies.

Secure and Auditable

Every action in the system is logged with a timestamp providing a full tamper-proof audit trail.

How It Works

1
Report

Citizen or officer submits a disaster report online or without an account.

2
Respond

Admin assigns a field responder who updates the status in real time.

3
Resolve

Incident is resolved and permanently archived for future reference.
